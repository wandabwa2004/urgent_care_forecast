# Article — From Forecast to Roster

The staffing-optimisation follow-up to *Predicting Daily Patient Volume for a
Melbourne Urgent Care Clinic*.

- **[from-forecast-to-roster.md](from-forecast-to-roster.md)** — the article draft,
  written for Medium / Data Science Collective.

## Where the figures come from

The article references results and charts produced by
`notebooks/06_staffing_optimization.ipynb` (executed end-to-end). Drop the rendered
plots from that notebook in next to the prose when publishing:

- demand distribution with the P90 plan line (§1 of the notebook)
- intraday arrival profile (§2)
- Erlang-C wait-probability curve at the peak hour (§3)
- required-doctors distribution across scenarios (§4)
- doctor coverage: required vs rostered (§5)
- sensitivity: cost vs consult time, doctors vs wait-SLA (§7)

## Reproducing the numbers

Every figure quoted in the article (94% coverage on the held-out 90 days, ~$13,600
mean daily cost, the consult-time and SLA sensitivities) is regenerated by running
that notebook against the committed `ml-pipeline/models/test_predictions.csv` and
`cv_residuals.npy`. The engine itself lives in `ml-pipeline/src/optimization/` and is
covered by `ml-pipeline/tests/test_staffing.py`.
